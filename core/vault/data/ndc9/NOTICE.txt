変更: 類目と綱目の分類項目名（skos:prefLabel）と、3桁の要目の文脈付き分類項目名（rdfs:label）だけを
labels.tsv に抜き出した。Book Props は要目を「007 情報科学」のように分類記号と名前を並べて表示し、
NDC10 や NDC8 などほかの版の分類記号にも、同じ3桁の NDC9 の名前を添える。
Changes: Only the preferred labels (skos:prefLabel) of the main classes and divisions and the contextual
labels (rdfs:label) of the three-digit sections are extracted into labels.tsv. Book Props shows a section
as its notation followed by its label, such as "007 情報科学", and also labels notations of other NDC
editions, such as NDC10 and NDC8, with the NDC9 name of the same three digits.
